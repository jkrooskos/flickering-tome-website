Privacy Policy

Flickering Tome Research · Effective September 20, 2026

Flickering Tome Research is an internal tool used to review and analyze the performance of Flickering Tome's eBay listings.

Information accessed

When authorized through eBay, the tool may access eBay seller analytics and listing-performance information permitted by the scopes granted to the application.

How information is used

The information is used only to analyze listing performance and support decisions about Flickering Tome's eBay listings. It is not sold or used for advertising to third parties.

Sharing

Information obtained through eBay is not sold or shared with third parties except as necessary to operate the tool or comply with applicable law.

Security and authorization

Authentication credentials and authorization tokens are treated as private and are not intentionally made public. Access is limited to the permissions granted through eBay. eBay authorization may be revoked through the user's eBay account.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the tool's use of eBay data changes.
